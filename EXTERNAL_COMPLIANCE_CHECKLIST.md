# Vertex Research Labs External Compliance Checklist

Use this before any website release and repeat quarterly and before each campaign/product launch. Treat every external statement, image, hashtag, link, comment, direct message, affiliate statement, and search snippet as part of the brand's total marketing impression. A bio/footer disclaimer does not cure contradictory content.

## Immediate hold and evidence preservation

- [ ] Pause new product, effects, “protocol,” transformation, restock-cadence, and personal-goal campaigns until review is complete.
- [ ] Preserve exports/screenshots of current and historical external content for counsel before deleting or editing regulated-content evidence.
- [ ] Identify the owner, access list, agency, and approval status of every account below.
- [ ] Create one approved-claims library tied to exact product/lot evidence; prohibit improvisation by staff, contractors, affiliates, and AI tools.
- [ ] Prohibit claims of “FDA compliant,” “FDA approved,” “legal,” “safe,” “effective,” “pharmaceutical grade,” “clinical grade,” “sterile,” or a purity/testing result unless counsel and evidence specifically approve the exact statement.

## Social accounts and communities

Audit Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X, YouTube, Reddit, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp groups, podcasts, livestreams, Linktree/beacons, and founder/staff accounts used for business.

- [ ] Search posts, captions, video/audio, on-screen text, alt text, thumbnails, filenames, hashtags, comments, replies, DMs, saved replies, pinned content, Stories/Highlights, bios, link pages, and deleted/scheduled drafts.
- [ ] Remove or counsel-review personal dosing, cycles, stacks, injection/route, reconstitution, syringes, bacteriostatic water, body-site diagrams, before/after content, personal testimonials, “what I take,” or instructions to evade medical/processor scrutiny.
- [ ] Remove or counsel-review weight loss, appetite, obesity, glucose/diabetes, muscle/recovery/healing, anti-aging/longevity, libido/sexual, sleep, cognition, mood, tanning, performance, disease, safety, efficacy, or side-effect claims.
- [ ] Do not use medical imagery, bodies, scales, gym transformations, syringes, clinicians, patient language, or consumer lifestyle imagery that implies personal use.
- [ ] Do not seed reviews or fabricate laboratory testimonials. Keep outcome testimonials disabled unless counsel approves a documented moderation program.
- [ ] Ensure replies to user questions refuse personal/veterinary recommendations and redirect only to neutral lot documentation or qualified institutional review.
- [ ] Remove coded names, euphemisms, emoji codes, “DM for menu,” alternate spelling, or redirect schemes that conceal product identity.
- [ ] Verify old cached posts, highlights, reposts, partner collabs, tagged posts, creator whitelisting ads, and ad-library entries.

## Paid search, SEO, directories, and AI/search surfaces

- [ ] Google Ads, Microsoft Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads and all remarketing audiences: remove consumer condition/effect keywords and creative.
- [ ] Google Business Profile, Merchant Center, Bing Places, Yelp, industry directories and marketplace listings: use accurate legal entity, category, product status and laboratory-only description.
- [ ] Search Console/Bing Webmaster Tools: submit the revised sitemap and request recrawl/removal of retired URLs where appropriate; monitor snippets and sitelinks.
- [ ] Search the web for brand + `dose`, `injection`, `reconstitution`, `weight loss`, `healing`, `muscle`, `libido`, `review`, `Reddit`, coupon codes, and every product name.
- [ ] Review historical title/description caches, image search, schema-rich results, third-party scraped catalogs, Internet archives with counsel, and AI answer citations.
- [ ] Do not bid on or create landing pages for “buy [drug],” “best peptide for…,” dosage, injection, prescription alternative, or health-condition queries.

## Email and SMS

- [ ] Inventory Resend audiences, automations, scheduled broadcasts, abandoned-cart flows, welcome series, delivery series, reward notices, win-back/restock/reorder flows, and one-off exports.
- [ ] Cancel queued reorder/restock/win-back messages before deployment; changing repository source does not cancel provider-side schedules.
- [ ] Review subject lines, preview text, body, images, product recommendations, dynamic blocks, links, UTM campaigns, footers, and forwarded templates for the same prohibited implications.
- [ ] Pause “time to reorder,” consumption-based cadence, “running low,” “mid-protocol,” “popular now,” autoship, and inferred-use recommendations pending counsel review.
- [ ] Separate transactional email from marketing; send marketing only under a recorded valid consent basis and honor unsubscribe/suppression immediately.
- [ ] Implement a working one-click unsubscribe where legally required; a mailto-only link may be insufficient for bulk commercial email requirements.
- [ ] Inventory SMS provider, number, consent language, double opt-in, quiet hours, STOP/HELP handling, carrier registration, and retained consent evidence. Do not send SMS marketing until complete.
- [ ] Escape user-provided names, notes, addresses, and product fields in HTML templates.

## Affiliates, influencers, referrals, and sales representatives

- [ ] List every code, commission, free-product relationship, ambassador, influencer, affiliate network, referral partner, clinic, reseller, and staff seller.
- [ ] Send written prohibited-claims rules and require preapproval of every post/script/link page; require prompt correction/takedown rights.
- [ ] Monitor affiliate content continuously, not only at onboarding. Archive evidence of review and enforcement.
- [ ] Require clear, unavoidable material-connection disclosures appropriate to each medium; hashtags alone may be inadequate.
- [ ] Prohibit personal outcome claims even when “honest,” unverified testing/quality claims, off-label framing, and instructions to contact privately.
- [ ] Suspend codes tied to noncompliant content. Review `PATRICIA10`, `ADAM10`, `JODI30`, `GLOWUP`, `SHAWN10`, `LISA10`, `KEN10`, `KLOW4LAURYN`, `ALEX30`, and referral links in current code/data.
- [ ] Review reward/referral structure with counsel; do not reward health-outcome testimonials, photo reviews, social claims, or consumer recruitment.

## Payment, merchant, commerce, and listings

- [ ] Obtain written acceptance of the exact business/product model from Stripe, banks, Apple Cash/Zelle or any replacement processor; do not miscategorize the business.
- [ ] Confirm merchant descriptor, legal seller, DBA registration, invoice identity, payee name, refund owner, tax collection, chargeback evidence, and prohibited-business policies align.
- [ ] Remove stale product feeds from Google Merchant Center, Meta catalogs, Shop apps, Stripe products/prices/payment links, affiliate feeds, and third-party storefronts.
- [ ] Disable any live autoship/subscription price, checkout link, coupon, abandoned checkout, or pay-by-link for withheld products.
- [ ] Confirm no staff member instructs customers to conceal product purpose or choose misleading payment notes.

## Product, packaging, fulfillment, and customer communications

- [ ] Counsel-review each retained material under actual sourcing/manufacturing/import/distribution facts before sale.
- [ ] Match every vial/carton/insert/packing slip/COA to exact product name, amount, lot, supplier, method, result, and required warnings.
- [ ] Quarantine the mismatched `ghk-cu-coa.jpg` and determine its actual product/lot; do not reuse it.
- [ ] Remove administration supplies, bacteriostatic water, syringes, needles, alcohol wipes, dosing calculators, preparation cards, or QR links to consumer instructions.
- [ ] Review support tickets, phone scripts, voicemail, chat macros, FAQs, invoices, order notes, refund reasons, and fulfillment comments for evidence of personal use.
- [ ] Adopt an escalation/refusal workflow when a customer reveals intended human/veterinary use. Preserve consistent records.
- [ ] Verify organizations rather than relying solely on a checkbox: institutional domain, legal name/address, responsible purchaser, intended laboratory context, sanctions/export checks where relevant.
- [ ] Define retention, quarantine, complaint, adverse-event/incident, recall, supplier qualification, deviation, and record-integrity procedures with qualified counsel/quality professionals.

## Privacy and security outside the repository

- [ ] Identify every Vercel, Supabase, Stripe, Resend, Google, EasyPost/USPS, domain/DNS, Git host, analytics, support, accounting, cloud-drive, and social account administrator; remove stale access and require MFA.
- [ ] Investigate exposure of customer exports in Git history, forks, CI artifacts, backups and clones; coordinate history purge and incident analysis before force-pushing.
- [ ] Review vendor DPAs/terms, retention, subprocessors, cross-border transfers, email open/click tracking, IP/device logging and deletion capabilities.
- [ ] Set and document retention periods by data class; implement deletion/anonymization jobs and legal holds.
- [ ] Test consumer/privacy request intake at `info@vertexresearchlabs.com`, identity verification, response tracking, and state appeal/opt-out procedures.
- [ ] Determine whether Global Privacy Control, “Do Not Sell or Share,” sensitive-data consent, or state-specific notices apply.
- [ ] Verify Google Analytics has no active legacy tag/container or server-side collection that bypasses the new site choice.
- [ ] Restrict publishable Google/Stripe/Supabase keys by domain/API/role as supported; rotate server secrets only after approved exposure analysis.

## Release sign-off

- [ ] Regulatory attorney approves catalog/product-by-product status and total intended-use posture.
- [ ] Advertising counsel approves substantiation, endorsements, email/SMS and state consumer-protection posture.
- [ ] Privacy counsel approves notice, consent/opt-out, retention and incident response.
- [ ] Payment processors approve the disclosed business and rails in writing.
- [ ] Quality owner signs off exact lot records and labels; no cross-lot inference.
- [ ] Security owner closes or accepts each HIGH item in `VERTEX_COMPLIANCE_AUDIT.md`.
- [ ] Deploy first to preview/staging; visually test desktop/mobile gate, catalog, every product, cart, checkout, legal pages, redirects, consent behavior, 404s and email rendering.
- [ ] Crawl the deployed preview for prohibited terms, stale pages, schema, sitemap and response codes.
- [ ] Deploy only after owner/legal sign-off; archive the approved snapshot and date.
- [ ] Re-audit the live site, CDN caches, Edge Function versions, Supabase policies, analytics requests, search results and scheduled provider campaigns after release.
